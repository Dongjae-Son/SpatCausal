## Data
Large datasets are stored on Dropbox due to file size: https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fo/ndzyiycrz946bv4i2hs7a/AMNGXTgBvI7cuUVtTxJ4c5A?rlkey=be6s5xolsuxl4azygtzt4zq29&st=bc46h985&dl=0

- Simulation results from Scenario 1 to 8 for the Naive, Shared, and Full models are available in the linked folder.
- Results from Scenario 1 to 8 for the PSA-B and PSA-G simulations can be found in the “propensity adjustment” folder.
- Results from Scenario 9 and 10 for the Naive, Shared, Full, PSA-B and PSA-G simulations can be found in the “new gamma” folder.

To check the bias, mean squared error, and 95% coverage probability, use the script 'posterior_analysis.R' in each folder.
